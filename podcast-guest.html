Pick a time. We'll handle the rest.

Thanks for saying yes. This calendar only shows times when both hosts, Allison Norris and Dr. Deren Flesher, are free, so whatever you pick is locked. The block is 60 minutes: a short warm-up, then we record. Video and audio.

How we record

In your browser, nothing to install and no account to make. Your link and the question we're taking on come by email from Deren within a day of booking, with a reminder the day before.

What to have

A laptop, not a phone, with Chrome or Edge. Wired headphones or earbuds. A quiet room with the door closed. A window or lamp in front of you, not behind you.

What you'll get

A short brief a week out: the question, the format and who's on the other side. After it publishes, the episode, the clips and a kit for sharing it.

Off the record

Say "off the record" and we stop. Nothing said off the record gets used. You'll also get a short release to sign before anything airs.
