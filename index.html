What an episode is like

A real conversation, not a panel.

No scripts, no slides, no PR answers. Two people who disagree, two hosts who push both of them, and enough time to actually get somewhere.

1

One question

Said plainly at the top, so nobody has to guess what we're talking about.

2

Two people who disagree

Not pundits. People who do this for a living and have already had this argument in real life.

3

A real conversation

Allison keeps it moving. Deren brings the numbers and the awkward question. Guests talk to each other, not past each other.

4

Where it landed

At the end, the hosts say what they actually think, what moved them, and what's still up in the air.
