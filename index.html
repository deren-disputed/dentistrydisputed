SEASON ONE

The first six questions.

Each one gets an episode: one question, two people who see it differently, and an honest conversation about it. No talking points, no PR answers.

01PUBLIC HEALTH

Should America keep fluoridating its water?

Seventy years of evidence on one side. State bans and a federal review on the other.

02BUSINESS

Is private equity saving dentistry or wrecking it?

Debt relief for new grads, or quotas and settlements. Both are true. Which one matters more?

03TECHNOLOGY

Should AI read your X-rays before a dentist does?

Consistent diagnosis, or dentists who stop looking and a liability nobody has priced.

04INSURANCE

Is dental insurance a scam?

The annual maximum hasn't moved since the 1970s. Thirty-seven states passed reform laws last year.

05WORKFORCE

Should hygienists work without a dentist in the building?

Sixty million Americans live where there aren't enough dentists. Who fills the gap?

06CLINICAL

Are we treating people who don't need it?

Fee for service rewards volume. Quotas reward volume. Where does the patient come in?

WHAT AN EPISODE IS LIKE

A real conversation, not a panel.

No scripts, no slides, no PR answers. Two people who disagree, two hosts who are curious, and enough time to actually get somewhere.

1

One question

Said plainly at the top, so nobody has to guess what we're talking about.

2

Two people who disagree

Not pundits. People who do this for a living and have already had this argument in real life.

3

A real conversation

Allison keeps it moving. Deren brings the numbers and the awkward question. Guests talk to each other, not past each other.

4

Where it landed

At the end, the hosts say what they actually think, what changed their mind, and what's still up in the air.
