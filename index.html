HOW AN EPISODE WORKS

Argue it properly.

Debate rules exist so the argument stays about the argument. Attack the position, not the person. Finish your sentences. Then steelman the other side.

FIRST

The motion

A stated, disputable claim goes on screen. Deren reads it. Each guest gets thirty seconds on who they are and which side they're on.

THEN

Openings

Four uninterrupted minutes each. "For" goes first. No slides, no scripts, no interruptions from the hosts.

THEN

The dispute

Allison leads the conversation. Deren asks the pressure question and brings the data. Guests can interrupt each other. Hosts referee.

LAST

Steelman and verdict

Each guest gives the strongest version of the other side's case. Ninety seconds to close. Both hosts say where the evidence landed and what's still open.
